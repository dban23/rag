# Aurora Brew 3000 - User Manual (Part 2: Controls & Operation)

## 1. The control panel

The control panel is located on the front of the machine, above the brew
chamber. It has four controls:

- **Power button** - Turns the machine on and off.
- **Brew-strength selector** - Toggles between *Mild*, *Regular*, and
  *Strong*.
- **Timer button** - Sets the auto-start time for your next brew.
- **Program button** - Confirms timer settings and shows the current time.

## 2. Brewing your first cup

1. Remove the carafe and lift the lid of the water reservoir.
2. Fill the reservoir with fresh, cold water up to the MAX fill line.
3. Open the filter basket and add one heaping scoop of ground coffee per
   cup, up to the basket's capacity.
4. Close the basket, place the carafe on the warming plate, and press the
   power button.
5. The machine will brew for approximately 8 minutes and then keep the
   carafe warm for up to 4 hours.

## 3. Using the programmable timer

To wake up to fresh coffee:

1. Press the Program button. The display will flash the current time.
2. Use the Timer button to advance to the desired brew time.
3. Press Program again to save. The display will show a small clock icon.

The machine will automatically start brewing at the programmed time as
long as the reservoir is full and coffee is loaded.

## 4. Pause-and-serve

You do not have to wait for the full brew cycle. Pull the carafe out at
any moment: a valve inside the brew chamber closes automatically and stops
the flow. You can pour a cup and return the carafe, and brewing resumes.
Do not remove the carafe for more than 30 seconds.

## 5. Adjusting brew strength

The brew-strength selector has three settings:

- **Mild** - A lighter body, recommended for larger cups.
- **Regular** - The standard balanced setting.
- **Strong** - A longer extraction for a bolder cup. Brew time increases
  to about 10 minutes on this setting.

## 6. First-time setup checklist

- Place the machine on a flat, stable surface.
- Remove all packing materials and stickers.
- Fill the reservoir and run one brew cycle with only water to rinse the
  system.
- Plug the machine in and set the clock with the Program button.
