Aurora Brew 3000 - User Manual (Part 1: Introduction & Safety)

Welcome to the Aurora Brew 3000, a premium automatic drip coffee machine
designed for home and small office use. This manual covers everything you
need to know to operate, clean, and maintain your machine.

1. Introduction

The Aurora Brew 3000 combines a 1.8 liter thermal carafe with a precision
brew chamber that holds up to 12 cups. Its stainless steel housing and
programmable timer make it ideal for households where everyone drinks
coffee at different times of the day.

Key features:
- 1.8 liter (60 oz) thermal carafe
- 12-cup capacity brew chamber
- Programmable 24-hour timer
- Built-in water filter indicator
- Auto shut-off after 2 hours
- Pause-and-serve function

2. Package contents

In the box you will find:
- Aurora Brew 3000 coffee machine
- 1.8 liter thermal carafe with lid
- Permanent gold-tone mesh filter
- Measuring scoop
- Descaler sample packet
- Quick start guide

3. Important safety instructions

Read all instructions before using the appliance.

- Place the machine on a flat, stable, heat-resistant surface.
- Do not operate the appliance with a damaged power cord.
- Keep the machine and its power cord away from children under 8 years.
- Never immerse the machine body, power cord, or plug in water or any
  other liquid.
- Use only the included carafe on the warming plate. The warming plate is
  designed to fit this carafe and no other.
- Unplug the machine when it is not in use and before cleaning.
- Do not use the machine if the lid or carafe shows visible cracks.
- Hot surfaces: the brew chamber, warming plate, and carafe become hot
  during use. Use handles and oven mitts.

4. Components overview

- Water reservoir: removable, 1.2 liter capacity, with MAX fill line.
- Brew chamber: where ground coffee sits; has a swing-out filter basket.
- Warming plate: keeps the carafe hot; auto shut-off activates after
  2 hours.
- Control panel: power button, brew-strength selector, timer, and
  program button.
- Carafe: thermal double-walled, keeps coffee hot for up to 4 hours.

5. Technical specifications

- Power: 1100 W
- Voltage: 220-240 V AC, 50-60 Hz
- Brew time: approximately 8 minutes for a full carafe
- Weight: 3.4 kg
- Dimensions: 33 x 24 x 38 cm
